# Pathline — 4–5 minute demo script

## Opening (30 seconds)

"Pathline is a career-switcher-focused hiring marketplace. Our core hypothesis is that recruiters may overlook qualified professionals because traditional job titles don't communicate transferable abilities. Rather than building another job board, we changed what recruiters evaluate first: a work accomplishment that demonstrates a relevant capability."

## Screen 1: Career direction (30 seconds)

"Meet Maya. She's been a Customer Success Manager for three years and wants to move into product management. Pathline starts with where she wants to go, rather than assuming her next role should look like her last one."

Action: Start with the prefilled example and click **Build my proof**.

## Screen 2: Guided work story (45 seconds)

"We ask Maya to share one accomplishment: the problem, what she personally did, and what happened. No full résumé rewrite or lengthy assessment required."

Action: Read the 25 customer interviews, engineering collaboration, and reduced support tickets. Click **Find transferable skills**.

## Screen 3: Transferable skills (45 seconds)

"The system makes suggestions from her words. Maya can remove skills that overstate her experience. We intentionally don't claim her skills have been independently verified."

Action: Unselect and optionally reselect a skill. Save proof.

## Screens 4–5: Discover and match explanation (60 seconds)

"Here are five clearly fictional sample jobs. Northstar needs product discovery and cross-functional work. Pathline traces the exact evidence behind those requirements, but also shows what hasn't been demonstrated—for example, roadmap ownership. We chose this instead of an opaque match percentage."

Action: Open **Associate Product Manager — Northstar Labs**. Point out the salary, supported evidence, unproven qualification. Click **Express interest** and submit the Proof-of-Work Card.

## Screens 6–7: Employer's view and connection (75 seconds)

"Now we switch to a simulated recruiter view. Notice that this candidate isn't introduced as a conventional PM. Her former job title is secondary to evidence of user research and collaboration. The recruiter still sees gaps and retains the decision. In the real product, identity is released with the candidate's consent after mutual interest."

Action: Click **Open recruiter demo** → **Invite to connect**. Send one message in the unlocked conversation.

"This is the connection moment: the recruiter has a reason to consider someone they might never have searched for. It is a simulation—we are demonstrating the workflow, not claiming real employer traction."

## Closing and tradeoffs (45 seconds)

"I deliberately didn't build a complex AI ranker, mass applications, skills testing, or a full ATS. Those add cost without helping test whether proof-first introductions change recruiter behavior. Our next validation would be a small concierge pilot: would hiring managers actually interview more relevant career switchers when shown concrete, contextualized work evidence? The key metric is qualified introductions converting into interviews."

## If someone challenges the matching model

"It uses a deterministic demo rule set, not real AI. I wanted to be transparent about limitations and devote engineering effort to a coherent, testable interaction. The capability we'd replace later is the skills-suggestion mechanism, after establishing whether the format is useful and safe."

## If someone challenges the cold start

"The five jobs, candidate, and recruiter are fictional and labeled. I would seed a real pilot with manually curated candidates and a few employers open to adjacent backgrounds, not create fake social proof."
